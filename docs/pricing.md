# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Starter to Pro or Team at any time.

---

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions can be canceled at any time.

---

## Do I keep my work if I downgrade?

Yes. Progress, photos and scores stay on your account. A downgrade changes which missions you can fly, never what you have already flown.

---

## Is there a student discount?

Yes.

Educational institutions can contact DroneVerse for special academic pricing.
